Pebbles Technologies ("we," "us," "our") is a Riyadh-based company operating across four lines of business: a marketplace and digital services platform, facility maintenance and operations, AeroWater atmospheric water systems, and premium chickpea trade. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

Depending on which service you use, we may collect:

  • Contact details you give us — name, email, phone number, company.
  • Messages you send through our contact form or email.
  • Account and booking details if you use the marketplace platform.
  • Site and installation details for facility-maintenance or AeroWater work.
  • Order and delivery details for chickpea trade customers.
  • Basic, aggregated site-analytics data (pages visited, general location, device type) — no individual tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use the information above to respond to enquiries, deliver the service you've requested, manage bookings and orders, meet our contractual and legal obligations, and improve our services. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, hosting or payment processing), with vendors or contractors directly involved in fulfilling a booking or order, and where required by Saudi law or a valid legal request.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by applicable law and regulation.

Your rights
