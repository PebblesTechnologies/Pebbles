About us

Two lines. One foundation.

Pebbles Technologies is a Riyadh-based venture built on a simple principle: durable, purpose-built solutions that work under real conditions — for water security and for digital commerce in the Kingdom.

Like the pebbles that give the company its name, each business line stands on its own weight while resting on the same foundation: local presence, practical delivery, and long-term reliability.

We are actively building capabilities in Reverse Osmosis and water treatment supply & services, and in e-commerce / multi-platform online commerce aligned with Vision 2030 digital growth.
